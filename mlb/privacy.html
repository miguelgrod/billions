Privacy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Playing collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. Nothing you do while playing is sent anywhere.

The leaderboard is the one exception, and only if you ask for it. Pressing Save score on the end screen — and only then — sends the name you type, along with the record of the game you just played, so the server can recompute your score. Nothing is sent if you don't press it. Your name and score are then public on the leaderboard; write to the address below to have an entry removed.

Where saved scores live. On Supabase, in Ireland (eu-west-1), the same region as the rest of the site. They are kept until you ask for them to be deleted.

No third-party requests. Every file the page needs — the styles, the font, the data — is served from this site. Your browser never contacts another company while you play, so no one else sees your IP address.

What stays on your device. Your best score is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data deletes it.

Your IP address. Should you save a score, the server keeps a salted one-way fingerprint of your IP address to curb abuse, never the address itself.

Hosting. The site is served from Amazon Web Services in Ireland (eu-west-1). Like any web server, it records standard access logs.

Data source. Player records, team records and player photographs come from the public MLB Stats API. The photographs are copyright of their owners and are used here for identification only. This game is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Major League Baseball or any of its clubs; team names are used descriptively to identify the franchises a player appeared for.
